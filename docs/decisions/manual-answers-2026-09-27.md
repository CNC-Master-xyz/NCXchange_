# Manual answers to the open drafts D108 to D346

Status: 2026-09-27; proposed from the maintainer's manual library; not confirmed. Every answer is a reading of the manuals, not a decision: the maintainer confirms or overrules each one, and only then does it move into `decisions.md`. The full answers, with their evidence, stand in the ANSWER blocks of `rationale.md`.

## How this was made

The library is the maintainer's own collection of controller and machine-builder documentation, `CNC Handbuecher Sorted`: 1642 PDFs in seven folders (534 control manuals, 944 machine-builder manuals, and metrology, automation, fundamentals, the maintainer's own notes and a rest folder). Every PDF was rendered once into a plain-text cache, one text file per PDF, so that a search could cross the whole library instead of one book at a time. The 239 open drafts of `rationale.md` were then cut into chunks of ten and given to one agent each: the agent searched the cache for the question, opened the pages the search found in the PDF itself, and wrote an ANSWER block under the draft. A second agent afterwards re-opened every citation of every answer, page by page, and corrected the page numbers, struck the claims the page does not carry, and added the pages the first pass had missed. Where the two passes disagreed, the corrected reading is the one recorded below.

The verdicts mean three different things. **Answered from a manual**: a page states the fact the draft asks for, and the answer is quoted from it. **Manuals do not settle it**: the question is an NCXchange choice, or no page states the rule, but the manuals bear on it and the answer says how. **Not in the library**: no controller or machine-builder document touches the question at all, and the draft's own recommendation stands unopposed. Confidence is the agent's own, over the reading rather than over the page.
