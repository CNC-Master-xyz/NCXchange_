D244 Fanuc G52 across G54..G59/G92, incremental words, under G68/G68.2/G51.1 — FACT WRONG (one point depends on the edition), the rest HOLDS, CITATIONS TO FIX

- (a) G52 sets the local system in all of G54..G59, with its origin given in workpiece coordinates, so a G55 keeps the shift. CONFIRMED: 30i-B Operator's Manual B-64484EN/05, p.216. Record cites GE p.216-217.
- G52 with a zero axis word puts that axis's local origin on the workpiece zero (caution 1); a new G52 names the new origin in workpiece coordinates. CONFIRMED: EN/05 p.216.
- Caution 4: G92 (G50 in system A) cancels the local system, except on the axes the block does not name. Cautions 5-6: G52 cancels radius compensation for a moment, and the next motion must be absolute. CONFIRMED: EN/05 p.217.
- (b) Lathe G52 U/W:
  - CONFIRMED: Nakamura-Tome Programmieranleitung Fanuc 31i Model A (WY series, 2008), p.51-52. The 2019 Nakamura Programmierhandbuch has the same text on p.52-53, and the Nakamura 18i-TB booklet on p.55-56. The origin is measured from the active G54..G59, whether written U/W or X/Z. U0./W0. clears the local system. G50 overwrites G52.
  - Small fix: the manual's example is "G52 U2.75 W1.75", not W1.25.
  - The Fanuc manuals themselves say nothing about U/W in G52.
- (c) G52..G59/G92 and G27..G30 forbidden in 2D G68 mode:
  - CONFIRMED: 30i-B milling volume B-64484EN-2, p.267. Also 0i-D milling EN B-64304EN-2-01, p.191. Record cites 0i-D GE p.266.
  - Alarm PS0412 is named only in the 0i-D text, not in the 30i-B text.
- Same prohibition in G51.1 mode. CONFIRMED: EN/05 p.368. Record cites GE p.374.
- G68.2 mode, G54..G59 and the 3TW parameter:
  - CONFIRMED: G54..G59 needs 1205#6 (3TW)=1, otherwise PS5462; with 3TW=1 the tilted plane's zero shift is kept. EN/05 p.890-891. Record cites p.870.
  - CONFIRMED: G92 is absent from the list of G codes allowed in G68.2 mode. EN/05 p.948-949.
- G52 in G68.2 mode — WRONG for the current edition:
  - EN/05 p.948-949 lists "Local coordinate system (G52)" among the G codes allowed in tilted working plane mode.
  - The German excerpt Fanuc_30i-B_Programmierung-Kap-5-Achsen-G68.2_B-64484GE, p.55-56, leaves G52 out, as the record says.
  - So "G52 raises PS5462 in G68.2 mode" depends on the edition. It is not a firm 30i-B rule.
- G51.1 under G68.2 and 3D conversion:
  - Both lists also allow G51.1 (milling). EN/05 p.948 adds that G51.1/G50.1 must sit inside G68.2: G68.2 first, then the modes on and off, then G69.
  - G51.1 is also allowed in 3D coordinate conversion mode. EN/05 p.345.
- 3D conversion (G68 with I J K) allows G28/G29/G30/G53 but not G52, G54..G59 or G92. CONFIRMED: EN/05 p.344-345. Record cites GE p.351.
- G53 may not share a block with G50/G51, G50.1/G51.1 or G68/G69. CONFIRMED: EN/05 p.202. Record cites p.201.
- (d) G28/G30 forbidden under 2D G68 and under G51.1, so main's HOME reading there is wrong. CONFIRMED: same pages as (c).
- What changes:
  - (c) holds for G68 and G51.1 but not as a fixed G68.2 rule. A G52 in G68.2 mode may be legal on the target control.
  - Keeping it RAW is still safe. The WARNING must not claim a control limitation, and fanuc.md 4 should name the edition dependence.
  - Since G51.1 may nest inside G68.2 and 3D conversion, "a G52 while G51.1 is active" and the cross cases need the G51.1-specific rule (EN/05 p.368), not the G68.2 one.

D213 JUMP out of a running REPEAT — HOLDS, CITATIONS OK; the conclusion is overstated

- CONFIRMED: Siemens 840D sl NC-Programmierung SW4.92 (2019), p.482. When jumps and part repeats are mixed, blocks run purely in sequence, and after a jump out of a repeat the control machines on until the programmed end of the part. The GOTOF example is on the same page.
- CONFIRMED: same manual, p.481. M17/RET aborts the repeat; a repeat occupies a subprogram level.
- CONFIRMED: TNC 640 Klartext 892903-18, p.277-278. Jumps do not take the LBL 0 return marks into account.
- Caveat: the Siemens example jumps to a label that still lies before the REPEAT line. The NCX case jumps past the REPEAT block itself, which the manual does not cover.
  - So "remaining passes stand" is an extrapolation.
  - The answer should say the manuals still do not settle the NCX case, rather than "rules against the recommendation".
- Extra searches for a Heidenhain REP-counter rule (TNC 640, 426/430, 310, 124, 128) found nothing.

D216 $SYS_POS_X / $SYS_MPOS_X under DIAMETER=ON — NEWLY (PARTLY) SETTLED BY THE MANUALS

- CONFIRMED: Siemens Programmierhandbuch Grundlagen 03/2013, p.169-170. Under DIAMON/DIAM90, WCS actual values read with MEAS, MEAW, $P_EP and $AA_IW are diameters. Same in SW4.92 p.168-171 and ONE p.166-169.
- CONFIRMED: Listenhandbuch Systemvariable SW4.5, p.477 ($AA_IW) and p.479 ($AA_IM). Neither entry mentions diameter.
- NEW: Siemens Funktionshandbuch Grundfunktionen (2012), p.901-902, "Fest Radius-bezogene Daten":
  - For facing axes, "Anzeigedaten bezogen auf das Maschinenkoordinatensystem" (machine-frame display data) are always radius values, as are offsets, frames, limits and feed.
  - MD20360 bits 6/7 can switch actual values and their display to diameter. p.902-903.
  - p.900-901 lists the DIAM90 diameter reads as $AA_MW, $P_EP, $AA_IW, all in the WCS.
  - So on a standard Siemens setup the machine-frame values are radius values, and $AA_IM very likely reads a radius. That is my inference: the page names display data, not the variable.
- Counter-hint: PRESETONS, a machine-frame value, takes active DIAMON into account. Sinumerik 840D sl Programmierung BS.000504, p.310-311.
- Fanuc #5021/#5041:
  - Table CONFIRMED: EN/05 p.488-489. Record cites GE p.469-470.
  - Still silent on diameter. Table 8.4(a) CONFIRMED at EN/05 p.230-231. Record cites GE p.231.
- What changes: $SYS_MPOS_X reading the diameter would need a factor of 2 in the Siemens reader and compiler for $AA_IM (unless MD20360 is set). The answer should say this instead of "silent".
