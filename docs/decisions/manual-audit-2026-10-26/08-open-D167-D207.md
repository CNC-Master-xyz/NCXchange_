### D177 Fanuc dwell P: HOLDS, CITATIONS TO FIX (plus one nuance)
- **Mill dwell P (CONFIRMED):** a dwell at the hole bottom only on G74, G82, G84, G88 and G89; nothing on G73, G81, G83 and G85. Source: 30i-B Bedienung-Fräsen B-64484EN-2, Table 5.1(a), p.56 (record: GE p.62).
- **Restrictions sections (CONFIRMED):** G82 has a P paragraph (p.67), G84 too (p.75). G73 (p.61) and G83 (p.69) have only Q, and G81 (p.66), G85 (p.76) and G86 (p.78) have neither. The example writes G82 … P1000 (p.68).
- **G89 is G85 plus a dwell (CONFIRMED):** p.83.
- **Lathe P (CONFIRMED):** G83/G84/G85/G87/G88/G89 all dwell at the bottom and the group has no G82. Source: Bedienung-Drehen B-64484EN-1, Table 4.3(a) p.92 and G83/G87 format p.95. The examples G84 … P500 and G85 … P500 are on p.98–99 (record: 96–101).
- **Nuance, new:** on a lathe with the Series 15 command format, the canned-cycle group is G81–G89 and does include G82 (Drehen EN-1 §6.5, p.297–299). In that format G83/G84 dwell only when P is in the block; in Series 15-T, G83 never dwells.
  - So "lathe DRILL with a dwell stays G83 with P" holds for the standard (16/18) format only.
- **Nuance, new:** on the mill, the optional small-hole peck drilling G83 (selected by an M code) does take P (Fräsen p.70). It is an option and does not change the answer.

### D181 SINUMERIK drilling cycles: HOLDS, ONE FACT TO ADD, 2008 CITATIONS UNVERIFIABLE
All in SINUMERIK 840D sl NC-Programmierung SW 4.92 (2019) unless noted.
- **CYCLE81 (CONFIRMED):** (RTP, RFP, SDIS, DP, DPR, DTB, _GMODE, _DMODE, _AMODE), DTB is the dwell at final depth (p.1082–1083).
- **CYCLE82 (CONFIRMED):** p.1083–1086.
- **CYCLE83 (CONFIRMED):** signature as cited (p.1086–1087). VARI 0 = Spänebrechen, 1 = Entspanen. FDEP is the first depth absolute and FDPR relative.
- **CYCLE84 (CONFIRMED):** p.1089. CYCLE85 with FFR/RFF and CYCLE86 (CONFIRMED): p.1092–1093.
- **CYCLE840 sl signature (CONFIRMED):** p.1129–1130. ENC=1 means the pitch comes from the programmed feed; 0, 11 and 20 take the pitch from MPIT/PIT.
- **MPIT (CONFIRMED):** ISO metric thread size, pitch computed at run time (p.1089, 1129).
- **Omitted parameter = 0 (CONFIRMED in a connector source):** same manual p.505, "Unvollständige Parameterübergabe" (the record cites the unreadable 2008 p.18). So an omitted VARI is 0, chip-break.
- **CYCLE830 (CONFIRMED, one fact to add):** S_FB is the Bohrvorschub (p.1121) and _DP depends on the ones digit of _AMODE (p.1120). But that digit is coded the other way round from the other cycles: 0 = incremental, 1 = absolute (p.1122–1123).
  - An omitted _AMODE therefore makes _DP incremental. CYCLE830's VARI also differs: 0 one cut, 1 chip-break, 2 peck, 3 both (p.1120).
- **Feeds (CONFIRMED in sl sources):** CYCLE84 taps with spindle/feed synchronisation at the programmed speed, no F. CYCLE840 computes F from speed and pitch. CYCLE81 and CYCLE83 use the programmed F. Sources: 840D sl Bedienhandbuch Fräsen 2013, p.304, 309, 315–316.
- **2008 Zyklen pages 18, 46–86 (NOT FOUND):** body not indexed; searched CYCLE84 G331 G332 SST1, MPIT 61001 and Versorgungsparameter. The facts are covered by the sl sources above.
- **Effect:** the maintainer's answer stands. The CYCLE830 depth mapping must use the inverted _AMODE coding.

- **Siemens CYCLE85 (WRONG for the sl):** the sl Bedienhandbuch says it retracts at FR all the way to the retract plane. Sources: Fräsen 2013 p.308 step 4, Drehen 2013 p.287. The record's 2008 p.73 (RFF to reference plane + SDIS, then G0) cannot be read in the connector.

- **New nuance:** on a Fanuc lathe in Series 15 format, G85/G89 go from Z to R at twice the cutting feed (Series 15-T: equal to it) (Drehen EN-1 p.299). That matters for change 1.
- **"Re-approach is not 0" (CONFIRMED):** the maintainer's answer already handles it through peck_clearance (D357).

### D192 F against max_feed: HOLDS, CITATIONS TO FIX; one Siemens page weakens the answer's last clause
- **Fanuc 1430 (CONFIRMED):** per-axis cutting feed limit; the interpolated axis component is clamped (Drehen-Fräsen EN p.178, record GE p.179). Unit mm/min, inch/min or deg/min (Fräsen EN-2 p.395).
- **C-axis clamp in normal direction control (CONFIRMED):** EN p.371 (record p.377).
- **1422 (CONFIRMED for 16i/18i):** common limit, with 1430 only for linear and circular interpolation. Source: 16-18-B Parameterhandbuch B-63530EN p.102–103.
  - The 30i-B parameter manual has no 1422 entry; there 1430 applies, or 1432 under AI or look-ahead control (Parameterhandbuch B-64490EN p.89). So the 1422 sentence should be labelled 16i, not "Fanuc".
- **Siemens p.273 (CONFIRMED):** Funktionshandbuch Grundfunktionen 2012, "an der Bahn beteiligten Achsen".
- **Siemens, newly found:** the same manual, p.1510, says the axis with the lowest maximum speed sets the maximum path speed. That is the Recommendation's path-feed reading, in Siemens' own words. The answer's clause "the Recommendation's path-feed reading is not the one the controls use either" holds for Fanuc only.
  - Same manual p.1509: by default the path feed counts only the programmed geometry axes (others only with FGROUP). That supports "rotary axes count only in rotary-only blocks".
- **Heidenhain (CONFIRMED):** CfgFeedLimits maxWorkFeed per axis in mm/min or °/min. TNC 640 Technisches Handbuch 892899-18 p.1951–1952 (record p.806–807).

### Answers that would change
1. **D190 (maintainer's answer):** change 2 does not hold for Siemens REAM on an 840D sl. CYCLE85 feeds out at FR to the retract plane (Bedienhandbuch 2013 p.308 and p.287). Either make the REAM retract target per control, or accept that a Siemens REAM differs. Optionally note the Fanuc lathe Series 15 rule (G85/G89 retract at twice F).
2. **D181 (maintainer's answer, by addition):** CYCLE830 _AMODE ones digit is 0 = incremental, 1 = absolute, the inverse of CYCLE81–86. CYCLE830 VARI has its own coding (0/1/2/3).
3. **D192 (proposed answer):** drop or qualify "the path-feed reading is not the one the controls use either". Siemens Funktionshandbuch p.1510 states the slowest participating axis bounds the path speed. Mark parameter 1422 as 16i/18i; 30i-B uses 1430 or 1432.
4. **D177 (proposed answer, wording):** the lathe "no G82" statement holds for the standard Series 16/18 format only. Series 15 format gives a lathe G81–G89 including G82.

Every other entry stands as written; only Fanuc citations need their document and page swapped to the English editions.
