General notes:
- **Fanuc documents.** The connector does not hold the German Fanuc 30i-B Betriebshandbuch B-64484GE/02 (it has only two short chapter extracts) or the German Parameterhandbuch B-64490GE/03. Every Fanuc 30i-B fact below was checked in the English B-64484EN and B-64490EN-05 instead.
  - Pages match up to about chapter 5 of the Betriebshandbuch and drift after that (chapter 23 is about +40 pages).
  - All German Fanuc page numbers in the record therefore need a document swap or a page fix.
- **Siemens alarm manual.** "Diagnosehandbuch 08/2018" in the record is the connector's 840D sl Parameter Alarme BS.000503. The 2005 840D Diagnosehandbuch is listed in the connector but returned no hits.

- **Own notes.** The "Eigene Notizen" files (Reiden post output, DMG L1001.txt) are not manuals. I did not check them.

## D286 Rest points under continuous: HOLDS, CITATIONS TO FIX
- **CONFIRMED** – Siemens implicit exact stop. Auxiliary function before motion, a block with no path motion, and buffer flush all end the previous block at the exact-stop criterion; there is also a second list of cases that brake to speed zero, including "eine Synchronisation". FB Grundfunktionen 2012, p.177.
- **CONFIRMED** – Overload factor: the velocity jump formula, and at factor 1.0 every non-tangential transition brakes to zero. FB Grundfunktionen 2012, p.178–179.
- **CONFIRMED** – Fast auxiliary functions are the exception; comment, computation and subprogram-call blocks do not disturb continuous mode; the no-blend list on p.316; G0 needs G60/G64 or else the machine-data default. PH Grundlagen 2013, p.312–313, 316–317.
- **CONFIRMED** – WAITM always ends the preceding block with exact stop; WAITMC brakes only if the partners are not yet at the mark. NC-Programmierung 06/2019, p.490–491.
- **CONFIRMED, wording slip** – G61/G64 table; G64 is the mode at power-on or **system clear**. The record says "after power-on and after reset". B-64484EN p.180–181.
- **CONFIRMED** – G00 waits for the in-position check (EN p.171). Dwell serves as exact check in G64 mode (EN p.189–190).
- **CONFIRMED, page to fix** – Waiting M code must stand in a block of its own and is not output to the PMC. B-64484EN §23.2, p.1037 (record cites GE p.997).
- **Citation to fix** – iTNC 530 M90: the record's "Klartext 340490-04 (2007), p.307" is not reachable in the connector. The same text (brief exact stop by default, transition arc with RR/RL) is in Benutzerhandbuch Klartext 340490 (2005), p.267, and DIN-ISO 340490-04, p.271.
- **NOT FOUND** – "Cycle 32 replaces M90 on the TNC 640". The TNC 640 manuals only list M90 as "an TNC 640 nicht erforderlich" (TNC640 Klartext 34059x-10, p.617–618). Searched: M90 + TNC 640.

## D290 Rotary-axis units in polar/cylinder plane: HOLDS, CITATIONS TO FIX
- **CONFIRMED** – 1420, 1430, 1660 and 1671 are axis data in mm/min, inch/min, degree/min (or /s²). 1671 = 0 means 1000 mm/s² or 100 deg/s². B-64490EN-05: 1420 near p.86, 1430 p.88, 1660/1671 p.100–101 (record cites GE p.89–92 and 106).
- **CONFIRMED** – Polar interpolation: the C component grows near the centre; 1430 triggers automatic override and clamp; the L/R definitions; the permitted G-code list has no G00. B-64484EN p.92–97 (record p.98, 100, 101).
- **NOT FOUND** – The π/180 orientation of the formula. The English text layer prints "formula-not-decoded". The record's glyph-coordinate argument cannot be checked through the connector.
- **CONFIRMED** – Cylindrical: feed is on the circumference; arc radius is in mm; positioning including G00 is forbidden. B-64484EN p.99–100.
- **CONFIRMED** – FGREF, default 1° = 1 mm. PH Grundlagen 2013, p.107–108.

## D305 Waiting for a finished or never-started channel: FACT WRONG (partly)
- **WRONG** – The answer says no page describes a control that releases a wait because the partner was never started. Nakamura Programmierhandbuch Band 1 (4810004ECL-1), p.127, says the opposite for three-unit machines: on WTS, NTM3 and NTY3, waiting M codes are **ignored** when one unit runs in automatic alone, and the next block is processed. So a builder's control does release a lone channel.
- **CONFIRMED, pages to fix** – Fanuc NOWT/NMWT. B-64484EN p.1037 caution 3; single-path check under handle retrace, EN p.1268 (record GE p.997 and p.1227).
- **NOT FOUND** – DMG Teil 2 X_GREIFER (no readable text in the connector). The same WAITM(0,…) before M30 pattern is readable in Teil 1 X_FUTTER_GAMMA, p.118–119.
- **Effect** – Keeping the ERROR becomes an NCX choice, not "what the machines do".

## D315 Mark of the generated SYNC: FACT WRONG / NEWLY SETTLED
- **WRONG (Siemens mark range)** – The answer says [0, 99] is documented as wrong at its lower bound. NC-Programmierung 06/2019, p.491–492, and Sinumerik ONE, p.483, say: a multi-channel system has at most 100 wait marks (0…99); a single-channel system has only mark 0.
  - So mark 0 is legal, and [0, 99] is exactly the documented range.
  - It also answers the "maximum number of markers" the answer could not find: 100.
  - The alarm 15000 text ("kleiner 1") is at BS.000503 p.285–286 (citation to fix). It looks like older wording; the current programming manual overrides it.
- **CONFIRMED** – 8110/8111 range "0, 100 to 99999999". B-64490EN-05, p.455.
- **CONFIRMED** – 11100 must lie outside the wait range. EN-05, p.578.
- **CONFIRMED** – Wait mechanics. B-64484EN p.1033–1037.
- **CONFIRMED** – M100–M197 and PS0160. Mori NTX1000SZM, p.284.
- **CONFIRMED** – M100P12 and M100P13 reuse. Nakamura Band 1, p.126–127.
- **CONFIRMED** – Post never exceeds M197. Mori ESPRIT post, p.93.
- **NOT FOUND** – ShopTurn 3G V1.0/V2.1 template and BARLOAD_SYNC mark 95 (no readable text in the connector). Searched: BARLOAD, WAITM(95,1,2), WAITMARKEN.
- **Supporting** – DMG GMX-CTX-TC Trainingshandbuch p.140–148 shows builder marks 10, 15, 20, 80, 90, 0, and also 8 and 22 in its subprograms. That supports a reserved-marks list.
- **Caveat** – The "every mark of M100–M199 is free on the NTJX" claim is too strong. The NTJX header template uses M198, and G196 predefines M199.
- **Caveat** – HMI-Advanced Bedienhandbuch 2008, p.275, warns that reusing a WAIT mark can confuse the synchronous display. This is display only, but it tempers "reuse is normal".
