2. Path 2 writes `T1212` without `G0`: turret 2 has no change template of its own, and the schema gives it none. Machine-config 3 and D219 say a holder other than default_holder needs no table; the reader reads its T words, and the compiler writes its changes, in the fixed turret form `T{tool:02}{offset:02}` (`FanucToolWords.TurretForm`). Giving it `G0` needs either a per-holder change template in the schema or a compiler rule that writes `G0` before the turret form on a Fanuc lathe; both are a decision beyond this fix. 4810004EEE p.67 says G00 "normally" stands with the T code because a T with wear offset may not complete under G99 without the spindle turning; the control accepts the bare T word.
